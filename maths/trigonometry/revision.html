06General solutions — appears every paper

sin θ = sin α
θ = nπ + (−1)ⁿα
cos θ = cos α
θ = 2nπ ± α
tan θ = tan α
θ = nπ + α
note
n ∈ ℤ, α = principal value. Never stop at α alone.
