10Hydrogen bonding ↳ notes

condition
H bonded to F/O/N only
types
intermolecular vs intramolecular
example
o- vs p-nitrophenol bp
Formal charge ≠ real charge — it ignores electronegativity, use only to rank Lewis structures.
π bonds are never counted in steric number / hybridization count.
Expanded octet only for period ≥3 elements — N, O, F never expand.
Lone pair contributes to shape's electron-pair geometry, not to the named molecular shape.
