10-second must-knows
W = Fd cosθ W = area under F-x graph W_net = ΔKE KE = ½mv² U_grav = mgh U_spring = ½kx² KE+PE = constant (conservative only) P = W/t = Fv cosθ v²_bot = v²_top + 4gR v_top,min = √(gR)

01Work by constant force↗

formula
W = F d cosθ
θ = force-displacement angle
sign
θ<90°: +ve · θ=90°: zero · θ>90°: −ve

02Work by variable force↗

formula
W = ∫F(x)dx = area under F-x
spring example
k=20N/m, x:0→4m ⟹ W=½×4×80=160J

03Work-Energy theorem↗

formula
W_net = ½mv² − ½mu² = ΔKE
net force/net work only

04Kinetic energy↗

formula
KE = ½mv²
v doubles ⟹ KE ×4
not this
KE ∝ v — WRONG, KE ∝ v²

05Potential energy↗

gravitational
U = mgh
spring
U = ½kx²
conservative
gravity, spring — path-independent, PE defined
non-conservative
friction — path-dependent, no PE, energy → heat
JEE extension — PE curve equilibrium
U=−A/rⁿ+B/rᴰ ⟹ dU/dr=0
equilibrium r & U_min કાઢવા derivative=0 ઉકેલો (PYQ solutions Q.3,Q.4 જુઓ)

06Conservation of mechanical energy↗

formula
KE + PE = constant
only if forces are conservative (no friction)
with friction
KE₁+PE₁ = KE₂+PE₂+|W_friction|

07Power↗

average
P = W/t
unit: watt (J/s); 1 hp=746W
instantaneous
P = F⃗·v⃗ = Fv cosθ
ચલ પાવર
W = ∫P dt
P-t ક્ષેત્રફળ; P = kt ⟹ W = kt²/2
પછી
½mv² = ½mu² + W

08Vertical circular motion↗

formula
v²_bot = v²_top + 4gR
height diff top→bottom = 2R
min speed at top
v_top,min = √(gR)
condition: string tension T=0

!Common mistakes

Object ન ખસે તો force ગમે તેટલું હોય, work = 0
Variable force નું work last-value×distance નહીં, area/integral વાપરો
W_net theorem ફક્ત net force માટે, single force માટે નહીં
KE+PE=constant ફક્ત friction ન હોય ત્યારે — friction હોય તો adjust કરો
Vertical circle height diff = 2R (diameter), R નહીં
